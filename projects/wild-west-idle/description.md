### 🎯 Description
If you are a fan of idle and arcade games, or perhaps you like adventure RPG games, our Wild West: RPG Idle Adventure game combines an exclusive combination of these genres. When monotony beckons you, whether at work or at home, just download our game and enjoy its fascinating gameplay. Take the place of a real hunter from the Wild West, going into the world of untamed animals waiting for your hunt!

### ⚡ Features
* Hunt on animals
* Buy new weapons
* Buy new buildings
* Upgrade the player
