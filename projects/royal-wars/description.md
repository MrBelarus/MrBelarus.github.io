### 🎯 Description
Your kingdom is under attack by formidable troops, and the fate of your realm hangs in the balance. The time has come to take action and protect your land from these relentless invaders.
Start training and uniting defenders to save your kingdom! Gather your forces, prepare your strategies, and get ready to face the enemy head-on. Your leadership and tactical skills will be crucial in this epic battle for survival.

### ⚡ Features
* Enemy waves system 
* Unique defense characters
* Merge characters
* Active skills
* Meta player upgrades
* Several biomes
