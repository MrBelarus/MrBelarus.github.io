### 🎯 Description
Welcome to the thrilling world of Survival Race, where speed meets strategy! Gear up for adrenaline-pumping races where your survival depends on your skill and ingenuity. Build your own ultimate racing machine by purchasing and combining a variety of car parts. Each component plays a crucial role in enhancing your vehicle's performance, so choose wisely to create the perfect blend of speed, agility, and durability.

### ⚡ Features
* Car inventory management
* Easy control your car on the spline road
* Upgrade items
* Active skills
* Meta player upgrades
* Several biomes
