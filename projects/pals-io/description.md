### 🎯 Description
Pals io is the ultimate mobile game for players hungering for a unique twist on the gameplay.
In an unexpected turn of events, pets have turned wild, leaving our hero, the lone stickman, to fend off the ferocious beasts that once brought joy to households. Dive into the world, where agility and strategy determine your success in this action-packed journey to reclaim peace.

### ⚡ Features
* Enemy waves system 
* Deep inventory system
* Collect best items
* Meta upgrades tree
* Active skills system
* Deep meta system
