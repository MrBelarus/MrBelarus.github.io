### 🎯 Description
Place powerful lasers and smartly positioned reflective blocks to destroy waves of incoming enemies!
Plan your strategy, bounce beams, and stop the invaders before they reach the goal.
Can you master the laser maze and hold the line?

### ⚡ Features
* Simple drag-and-drop controls
* Dozens of tactical combinations using mirrors, splitters, and more
* Enemy waves system
* Blocks upgrades
* Meta player upgrades
* Several biomes
