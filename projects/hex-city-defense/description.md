### 🎯 Description
Welcome to the world of Hex City Defense, a unique strategy game where you have to build and defend your own hexagonal city from the onslaught of enemies! In this exciting mobile game, you can develop your city, expanding its borders and unlocking new types of buildings that will help strengthen your defense.
Each building has its own unique features and capabilities. To cope with waves of enemies, choose special skills that enhance the defensive characteristics of your buildings. Explore various tactics and strategies to create an invincible defense!
Gather resources, improve your infrastructure and do not forget to watch out for enemies who can attack at any time. Use your wits to organize your defense wisely and avoid falling under the onslaught of rivals.

### ⚡ Features
* Enemy waves system 
* Hex grid system
* A lot unique defense buildings
* Deep meta system
