### 🎯 Description
Ever dreamed of running your own guns factories? 
Show your skills as a manager, invest wisely in staff and factories, and work your socks off to become a guns factory tycoon in this addictive and entertaining casual simulator. Start from the ground up in this fun and fast-paced time-management game.

### ⚡ Features
* Top-down tycoon game
* Buy factories and upgrade them
* Clients system
* Deliver items to factories and sell points
* Player upgrades
